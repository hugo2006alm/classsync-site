One quiet pipeline

Your lecture finishes. ClassSync takes it from there.

The client keeps the useful data and credentials on your device. The relay exists only to wake devices up and coordinate work safely.

01

Discover

Fireflies finishes transcription. ClassSync discovers the meeting through the relay or recovery polling and fetches the transcript directly.

02

Understand

Gemini matches the lecture against your active subjects and turns raw speech into structured Portuguese study notes.

03

Publish

The note lands in your Notion summary database, linked to the right subject and protected against duplicate publishing.

Privacy by architecture

The relay never needs your lecture.

Transcripts, summaries and Notion content stay between your device and the services you chose. The Cloudflare relay stores identifiers, timestamps and encrypted account sync data — not lecture text.

What stays where

Credentials → OS secure storage Queue → local SQLite Transcripts → device Summaries → Notion Wake-up metadata → relay
