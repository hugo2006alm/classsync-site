v0.3.25 · Optional ISEP sources

New accounts can leave ISEP Portal and Moodle off during setup. The same switch is available under Settings → Connections. While off, ClassSync hides imported Academic sections and data, stops ISEP refreshes, and keeps local tasks and manual evaluations available. Existing accounts keep their previous enabled behavior after upgrading.
